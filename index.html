How it works & tips

Tap Listen to hear a native-style voice, then tap the mic and say the phrase. Words we recognised turn green; words we didn't catch turn red. Tap any word to hear it on its own.

The score is based on how closely the speech engine's transcript matches the target phrase, weighted by its confidence. It's a good guide to intelligibility, not a phonetics lab — a native speaker is still the gold standard.

Works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari (including iPhone). Firefox doesn't support speech recognition. Use a quiet room and speak at a natural pace.

Your voice is processed by your browser's built-in speech service. Nothing is stored except your scores, on this device.
