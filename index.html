Why we built Harley Says

My wife lives in the UK and is always looking for ways to practise her pronunciation. Classes help, but in between it's hard to find someone patient enough to listen, say it back properly, and tell you honestly how you sounded.

So we built the tool we wished existed: free, no sign-up, and ready whenever you are.

Why Harley?

The app is named after Harley, our much-loved bichon frise. In the app, Harley is learning to talk too, so every time you practise, you're practising together.

Neil

How it works & tips

Tap Listen to hear a native-style voice, then tap the mic and say the phrase. Words we recognised turn green; words we didn't catch turn red. Tap any word to hear it on its own.

The score is based on how closely the speech engine's transcript matches the target phrase, weighted by its confidence. It's a good guide to intelligibility, not a phonetics lab — a native speaker is still the gold standard.

Works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari (including iPhone). Firefox doesn't support speech recognition. Use a quiet room and speak at a natural pace.

Your voice is processed by your browser's built-in speech service. Your scores stay on this device. If you join the Premium waitlist, we keep your email only to tell you about the launch.
