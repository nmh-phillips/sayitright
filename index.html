    Why we built Harley Says

    My wife lives in the UK and is always looking for ways to practise her pronunciation. Classes help, but in between it's hard to find someone patient enough to listen, say it back properly, and tell you honestly how you sounded.

    So we built the tool we wished existed: free, no sign-up, and ready whenever you are.

    Why Harley?

    The app is named after Harley, our much-loved bichon frise. In the app, he's learning to talk too, so every time you practise, you're practising together.

    Neil
