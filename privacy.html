Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name.
  • Your voice recordings are sent to Microsoft only to be scored. We don't keep them.
  • Your scores and progress stay on your own device.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • We only have your email if you give it to us (feedback or the Premium waitlist).

2. Who Harley Says is for

Harley Says is for adults aged 18 and over. Please don't use it if you're under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has sent us personal data, we'll delete it.

3. What we process, and why

Your voice, when you're scored

When you tap to say a phrase, use "Each sound", or read a text in "Assess me", your recording is sent securely to Microsoft Azure AI Speech, which listens to it and sends back a score for each word and sound. Harley Says doesn't store your recordings, and Microsoft doesn't keep the audio after it has scored it.

Why: it's how the app does what you asked it to (scoring your pronunciation). This is our legitimate interest in providing the service, and it's needed to provide it to you.

Your voice, in the quick check, "Speak freely" and dictation

These features use the speech recognition built into your web browser. Depending on your browser, the audio may be processed by the browser's maker (for example Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, or Microsoft for Edge) under their own privacy policies. We only receive the resulting text.

Text for "Assess me" and the coach (Harley or Lilou explains)

For "Speak freely", "Write" and coaching tips, the text involved (what you said or wrote, the phrase, and your scores) is sent to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI, to write your feedback. No audio is sent to Anthropic. Anthropic does not use this data to train its AI models. Please don't type personal details (yours or anyone else's) into "Write".

To read a tip aloud, its text is sent to Microsoft Azure's text-to-speech service. Common phrases and Harley's barks are saved as audio so they load faster; these contain nothing about you.

Translations ("My phrase")

When you translate a phrase, the text is sent to DeepL (Germany) to translate it, or to Anthropic if DeepL isn't available.

Daily free allowances

To share the free daily allowances fairly and stop misuse, the app creates a random code stored in your browser (your "device ID"). Our server turns that code and your IP address into scrambled one-way codes (hashes) and counts how many times each is used per day. We can't work out who you are from them. Daily counts are deleted automatically within about a week.

Why: our legitimate interests in preventing abuse and keeping the free service affordable.

Anonymous usage totals

We count how often each feature and language is used each day (for example "120 visits, 40 in Spanish"). This uses no cookies, IP addresses or IDs, so it can't identify anyone.

Feedback

If you send feedback, we receive your message, the languages and topic you were practising, your browser type and, if you choose to give it, your email address. We use it to improve the app and to reply to you. We keep it for up to 2 years, or less if you ask us to delete it.

Premium waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address and chosen language only to tell you when Premium launches. You can ask to be removed at any time, and we'll delete the list once it's no longer needed.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify briefly processes your IP address and basic technical information to deliver pages and protect the site.

4. What's stored on your device

The app saves your settings (languages, coach, voice), your scores and progress, your device ID and your remaining free allowances in your browser's local storage. If you add Harley Says to your home screen, your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files so it opens quickly. Everything stays on your device and is needed for the app to work, so we don't use a cookie banner. You can delete it all by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Your scores, level estimates and tips are produced automatically by AI. They're a learning aid only and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. Because we hold very little that's linked to you (mainly an email address, if you've given us one), the quickest way to exercise these rights is to email hello@harleysays.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or the data protection authority where you live.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you have similar rights to know, delete and correct your data, and we don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our service keys are kept on the server and never reach your browser, and we keep as little data as we can.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant (for example when Premium accounts launch), we'll make it clear in the app.
